How to request account deletion

Users can request account deletion inside the Bit Byte - HRMS mobile application:

Profile / Settings → Account → Delete Account
  1. Open the Bit Byte - HRMS mobile application.
  2. Go to Profile / Settings.
  3. Open Account.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the account deletion request.
Alternative Request Method:
If the user cannot access the mobile application, they can contact their organisation's HR or administrator to request account deletion.

Data deletion and retention

Account-related personal data will be deleted after the deletion request is verified.

Some records may be retained where required for legal, payroll, attendance, audit, security, or compliance purposes.

Data that may be deleted:

  • User profile information
  • Personal account credentials and login sessions
  • Contact information (email, phone)
  • Profile photo and app preferences
  • Other personal information not required to be retained

Data that may be retained:

  • Attendance records (statutory requirement)
  • Payroll, salary, and taxation records
  • Employment records and contracts
  • Statutory compliance records
  • Security audit logs and access history
“Certain records may be retained where required for legal, payroll, taxation, statutory, audit, employment, or regulatory purposes. Retained records will only be kept for the period required by applicable law or legitimate organizational obligations.”
